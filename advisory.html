The Integrated Leader
Cedar Wave Advisory serves the modern executive in their pursuit of fully integrated leadership — of their lives, their organizations, or both.

The integrated leader seeks to steward all areas of their domain with intentionality, authenticity, and purpose. These leaders:

  • Earn their authority through character, integrity, and influence — not title or org chart position
  • Seek to shrink the gap between who they are on the inside and how they act on the outside
  • Focus not just on output, productivity, and KPIs, but on the people driving those metrics
  • Lead their organizations without compromising faith, family, friendships, or other areas of importance
  • Serve their families and communities with their unique gifts, personalities, and passions

This type of leadership is hard. But the path and pursuit is worth it.

The integrated leader is the leader of the future.

"AI will build many companies, increase our capabilities, and expand our territories. But it cannot manufacture character, form identity, or defend a culture.

Only leaders can do that."

High level leadership comes with many challenges, demands, pressures, and isolation. You don't have to walk it alone.

The Cupbearer

In antiquity, a king's cupbearer was his most trusted advisor — a paid position of the highest order. He didn't manage the calendar or command the troops — he made sure the king wasn't being poisoned or distracted from the outside, so he could lead with clarity from the inside.

Today, poison may not enter the same way, but it is present nonetheless, finding its way through distraction, isolation, mounting pressure, and an incredibly hurried pace.

These leaders need the wisdom and discernment of a trusted confidant like never before.

How Engagements Unfold

Most engagements begin with a 90-day arc — three months, two sessions each month, with support and resources between. After that, continuation is month-to-month, based on what's useful and what makes sense for both of us.

01 Listen & Diagnose
The first month is mostly intake. Sessions in this stage are focused on understanding your read of things — the people, the dynamics, the culture, or if the work is more personal, what you're navigating, any tension or pain you're carrying, and where you hope to go. I'll send a short set of reflection prompts before our first session to help frame the conversation. At the end of the month, I'll offer a written synthesis of what I'm hearing — themes, tensions, patterns, and the questions I think deserve attention.
02 Prioritize & Advise
With a clearer picture, we turn to what most needs attention. I'll offer perspective, guide you towards clarity on what needs to shift or change, and help you think through options before you act. Between sessions I typically send a thought exercise or resource relevant to what we're working through — something to sit with at your own pace.
03 Pressure Test & Plan
This stage is about testing the thinking that's developed. What holds? What are you still not seeing clearly? What decisions are on the horizon that deserve a second set of eyes before you commit? We'll have a clear sense of what you're building toward — whether personal or organizational — and how to sustain it. We'll also use this time to assess honestly whether continued engagement makes sense for both of us.
